# The Mountjoys of Barkly Oost — identified
Researched 9 September 2026. **The lead is resolved, and it adds a whole branch.**

## The lead
Reading the 1889 Doop Register of **Barkly Oost** for a Booyzen baptism, the archive noticed — on the
**facing page**, three weeks later — an entry it had no reason to expect:

> **6 October 1889 · George**, geb. 21 Julie 1889.
> *Ouders:* **Hercules Johannes MOUNTJOY** and **Maria Sophia van Niekerk**.
> *Getuigen:* Jan Dirk Abraham Francois van Niekerk · Isabella Margaretha van Niekerk ·
> **Jakobus Jonathan MOUNTJOY** · Maria Kuny · **Maria Sophia MOUNTJOY**.

This line descends from **Johanna Catharina Mountjoy (1819–1863)**, who married Petrus Jacobus Booysen at
Cradock in 1838 — and of whose family the archive had little more than a surname and a father who died in
1827. Three named Mountjoys, in the right district, twenty-six years after her death.

### It fits to the month
Hercules Johannes married **8 October 1888 at Dordrecht**; his first child **George was born 21 July
1889** and baptised at Barkly Oost on **6 October 1889**. FamilySearch independently records that child
as **George Mountjoy, 1889–1963**.

## The finding
> **The Mountjoys of Barkly Oost are the descendants of Johanna Catharina Mountjoy's brother.**

**George Mountjoy, saddle maker of Cradock**, was her younger brother. His sons **James Jonathan** and
**Hercules Johannes** are the two Mountjoy men on that register page — witness and father.

And the closeness is startling once you set the two baptisms side by side:

| | Booyzen | Mountjoy |
|---|---|---|
| Child | **Johanna Catharina Booyzen**, b. 20 June 1889 | **George Mountjoy**, b. 21 July 1889 |
| Baptised | **15 September 1889**, Barkly Oost | **6 October 1889**, Barkly Oost |
| Father | Willem Hermanus Booysen | Hercules Johannes Mountjoy |
| Godparents | **John Barry and Margaret W. E. Kolby** (her grandparents) | van Niekerk and Mountjoy kin |

**The two children were second cousins, born a month apart and baptised three weeks apart in the same
church.** Their fathers were first cousins. And the Booyzen daughter was named **Johanna Catharina** —
after the great-aunt who is the link between them.

## What it adds to the archive
1. **A whole collateral branch**, with thirteen children in the 1848–1878 generation and nine in the next.
2. **George Mountjoy's occupation: SADDLE MAKER** — the first trade recorded for a Mountjoy here.
3. **The parents' marriage: 9 March 1817, Graaff-Reinet** — new, and it places James Mountjoy and Anna
   Catharina at Graaff-Reinet two years before Johanna Catharina's birth there.
4. **The Mountjoys stayed.** James Jonathan died at **Barkly East** in 1937; Hercules Johannes in
   **Wodehouse** in 1941; Maria Sophia van Niekerk lived to **1961**. While the Booyzens went to
   Johannesburg, their Mountjoy cousins remained in the district for another half-century.

## Names crossing between the families
- **Johanna Catharina** — George named a daughter so in **1860**; his sister's grandson's family named one
  so in **1889**. The same name on both branches, from the same source.
- **Willem Hermanus** — a Booysen name, and **George Mountjoy named a son Willem Hermanus in 1872**;
  Hercules Johannes named another in **1915**.
- **Maria Sophia** — Botha, then van Niekerk, then a Mountjoy daughter in 1898. The witness
  *"Maria Sophia Mountjoy"* at the 1889 font is most likely **Maria Sophia Botha (1827–1911)**, the
  child's grandmother, then 62. <span>Inferred</span>
