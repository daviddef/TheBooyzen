## The Mountjoy line
**Johanna Catharina Mountjoy (1819–1863)** — m. Petrus Jacobus Booysen, 4 Nov 1838, Cradock
- b. 29 Apr 1819, **Graaff-Reinet**; bapt. 16 May 1819, Graaff-Reinet
- d. **19 May 1863, Zevenfontein**, aged 44
  - src: FamilySearch, Cape Province Probate Records, ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSQF-XSMD-B
- MyHeritage flags **3 conflicting birth facts** (two 1819 Graaff-Reinet, one 11 Jul 1822 Cradock)
- MyHeritage flags **Stephanus Francois b. 1875, twelve years after her death** — and Izak b. 1864
  is also after it. ⇒ **Petrus Jacobus remarried; the second wife is missing from the tree.**
  Those two youngest "sons" are almost certainly by an unrecorded second marriage.
- Her parents: **James Mountjoy (1786–1827)** m. **Anna Catharina Schleher (1787–1885)** — another
  **German** surname, and she lived to **98**.
- Her half-brothers are **Pietersen** (b. 1808–1813) ⇒ Anna Catharina Schleher was married to a
  Pietersen before James Mountjoy.
- Mountjoy siblings, and the infant mortality is stark: Thomas Jacobus (1818–1818), Jacoba Wilhelmina
  (1820–1856), Anna Christina (1822–), George (1823–), **James (1826–1826)**, **Willem Jan (1828–1828)**
  — the last born the year after his father died.

So the maternal lines of this family are **German three times over**: Kolbe (Württemberg),
Schleher, and — through Larman/Kohlbeck — the German Lutheran community of Soho.
