# The cousin marriage — Catherine Booyzen descends from one couple twice

When **George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen** married **Anna Johanna Maria Pretorius** at Vereeniging on
30 December 1924, they were not strangers who happened to share a district. **They were cousins**, and
their marriage folds the family tree back on itself.

**Kathleen Barry and Hendrik Nicholaas Barry were brother and sister.** George was Kathleen's son;
Anna was Hendrik's granddaughter. **George and Anna were therefore first cousins once removed**, and
their common ancestors are **John Augustus Barry I and Margaretha Kolbe**.

## What follows from it
**Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen (1929–2013) descends from John Augustus Barry I and Margaretha Kolbe
along two separate paths** — through her father *and* through her mother. This is **pedigree collapse**:
she has fewer distinct ancestors than the arithmetic of generations would predict.

It also means the **1820 Settler Kolbe descent, and the Irish Barry descent, each reach her twice.**

## It was not an isolated match — the two families married each other repeatedly

| (via Pretorius) Anna Johanna Maria Pretorius (1905–1944) | → | George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen (1901–1947) | 30 Dec 1924, Vereeniging |

Four Barry–Booyzen unions across three generations. In a district the size of Barkly East that is not
remarkable — it is how a small settler population reproduced itself. But it should be stated plainly,
because it changes how any DNA result from this line must be read.

## Anna Johanna Maria Pretorius (1905–1944)
- b. **3 Oct 1905, Barkly East** — the same month her infant brother **Hendrik Nikolaas died (26 Aug 1905)**
- **"Named after: Anna Johanna Maria Schoemann"** — her own grandmother, recorded explicitly in the tree
- d. **26 Feb 1944, Forest Hill, Johannesburg**, aged 38 —
  **cause of death recorded as "FALLOPIAN TUBE PREGNANCY"** (an ectopic pregnancy)
  - src: FamilySearch "Transvaal" ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS9R-Y9LN-M image 345/2754
- She left six children, the eldest 18 and the youngest 10. Her husband George remarried
  (Gertruida Alexandra Kinnear), lost two infant daughters, and died himself three years later.
  **Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen was orphaned at 17.**

## Her mother, Margarietha Wilhelmina Elizabeth Barry (1880–1961)
- b. **16 Mar 1880, Barkly East, Wodehouse, Cape Colony**; christened **12 Jun 1880 at Paarl**
- Occupation recorded: **Housewife**
- m. **8 July 1901** — *in the middle of the Anglo-Boer War* — **"at the home of D Schoeman,
  'Caerlaverock', Barkly East district"**. The venue is her mother's Schoemann family; the farm carries
  a **Scottish** name (Caerlaverock, Dumfriesshire).
- d. **1 Apr 1961, Germiston**; buried **Primrose Cemetery**
- Her husband **Petrus Jacobus Pretorius** d. 23 Apr 1941 at **96 James Street, Germiston**
