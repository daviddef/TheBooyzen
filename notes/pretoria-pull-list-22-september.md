# For Anne — National Archives Repository, Pretoria, Tuesday 22 September 2026

**Eight estate files. If you can only get five, the first five are the five.**

The National Archives Repository, 24 Hamilton Street, Arcadia, Pretoria.
Public access to records **older than twenty years is free of charge** — every file below is.
Reading Room hours are normally 08:00–16:00, and requisitions usually stop mid-afternoon, so
**hand the slips in as early as you can**; some depots also cap how many files you may have out
at once, which is why this list is ranked.

**The series is `MHG` — Master of the Supreme Court, Transvaal — under depot code `TAB`.**
Write the reference exactly as given. If a counter asks for a source code, it is **MHG**.

---

## What to photograph, in every file

1. **The death notice (`sterfkennis`)** — **this is the page that matters most.** It names the
   dead person's **father and mother**, their birthplace, occupation, the surviving spouse and
   **the list of children**. If you photograph one page per file, photograph this one.
2. The **liquidation and distribution account**, if there is one.
3. The **will**, if there is one.
4. **The file cover**, with the reference visible — so we can prove which file the pages came from.

Both sides of anything written on both sides. Flat, in focus, whole page in frame including the
edges. If the writing is faint, one extra shot closer in. **Don't retype anything** — a photograph
of a bad hand is worth more than a good transcription of it.

## The eight, ranked

### 1 · TAB, MHG 3734/46 — DASCHNER, CATHERINE MARY SOPHIA, born BOOYZEN (1946)
Born 22 April 1891, baptised at Barkly Oost; married Joseph Heinrich Daschner at Indwe.
**We want her parents' names.** Her husband's file (MHG 1928/42) has been read and does not
give them. This is the single most useful file on the list.

### 2 · TAB, MHG 40293 — KOLBE, FREDERICK FORTUNATUS DOWNING (1918)
**Never read, and it may settle the oldest question in the whole archive.** "Downing" is carried
here as a *forename* — it was the maiden name of Margaret Downing, who married the 1820 Settler
George Augustus Kolbe in London in 1819. **If his death notice names his father, it may connect
the Transvaal Kolbes to the Settler directly**, which everything else in this archive currently
reaches only by inference. Please treat this one as equal first if the queue allows.

### 3 · TAB, MHG 1095/48 — BOOYZEN, GEORGE DOWNING MOUNTJOY (1947)
Died 11 December 1947 at Forest Hill, Johannesburg; a clerk with the South African Railways.
His wife's file (MHG 2945/44) has been read; his has not.

### 4 · TAB, MHG 769/52 — BOOYZEN, JOHN BARRY (1952)
Born about 1884. **He signed his father's death registration at Indwe in 1905** — so he was
literate, present, and old enough to be a witness. His own file has never been opened.

### 5 · TAB, MHG 10/57 — BOOYZEN, HENRY JAMES (1956)
The man whose cause of death this archive got wrong twice and corrected from a pension form.

### 6 · TAB, MHG 5155/51 — BOOYZEN, GERT JACOBUS (1951)
Born 19 January 1870; married Isabella Frederika Coetsee at Middelburg in 1896.

### 7 · TAB, MHG 5450/68 — ROUSSEAU, surviving spouse MARIA MARGARETHA DOWNING ROUSSEAU (1968)
Index entry only. Another carrier of the Downing forename, two generations on.

### 8 · TAB, MHG 52653 — BOOYZEN, JACOBUS NICHOLS (1923)
**The reference may be wrong.** He is the eldest of the eight children named on an 1863 Cape
death notice, and this is the one file of the set that could not be found on film at all. The
1923 numbers in this series do not look like the later ones, so **if the slip comes back "not
found", please ask whether there is an MHG index volume or a card index for 1923 and look up
BOOYZEN in it** — the right reference matters more to us than this particular file does.

## If a file comes back empty or missing

That is worth knowing and **please write it down** — which reference, and what the staff said.
This archive publishes what it could not find at the same length as what it could, so a "not
found" with a reason is a real result and not a wasted slip.

## If there is any time left over

Ask at the counter whether the repository holds an **MHG index for the 1920s** that can be
browsed, and whether **BOOYZEN, BARRY, MOUNTJOY, DASCHNER or KOLBE** appear in it beyond the
references above. We have swept the online index (NAAIRS); we have never seen the paper one, and
paper indexes routinely hold entries the database does not.
