# Documents transcribed from the MyHeritage photo store

Extracted 9 September 2026 from the MyHeritage tree *Defranceski Family Site (23andMe)*, at full
resolution, and read directly. Files are in `sources/booyzen-photos/`.

**These are not family snapshots. They are primary civil records** — death notices, death registrations,
a marriage register and birth entries — scanned and attached to the tree by an earlier researcher, and
never transcribed. Reading them changes several of the archive's conclusions.

---

## 1. Death registration of WILLEM HERMANUS BOOYSEN, 1905
`sources/booyzen-photos/willem-hermanus/willem-hermanus-death-1905.jpg` (1764 × 1684)

**"FORM OF INFORMATION OF A DEATH: ACT No. 7 OF 1894"** — Cape Colony. Entry No. 299.

| Field | As written |
|---|---|
| 1. Christian Names and Surname | **Willem Hermanus Booysen** |
| 2. Sex | Male |
| 3. Usual place of Residence | **Doorn Street, Indwe** |
| 4. Age | **Fifty Five years** |
| 5. Race | European |
| 6. Condition | Married |
| 7. **Occupation** | **MASON** |
| 8. **Date of Death** | **September Thirteenth 1905** |
| 9. Place of Death | Doorn Street, Indwe |
| 10. Intended Place of Burial | **Public Cemetery, Indwe** |
| 11. **Causes of Death** | **ULCER OF STOMACH** |
| 11A. Duration of last Illness | **Fourteen months** |
| 12. Medical Man's Name | **W. C. Macdonald, M.B.** |
| 13. Informant's signature | **J B Booyzen** |
| 14. Qualification | **Present at Death** |
| 15. Residence | Doorn Street, Indwe |
| Registered | **14 September 1905**, sub-district **Indwe**, district **Wodehouse** |
| (b) total children | **Twelve** |

### What it settles
1. **The 1905 death date is now DOCUMENTED.** The tree's annotation *"To be confirmed, not sure"* can be
   retired. He died **13 September 1905 at Indwe**.
2. **He was not shot by the British.** He died of a **stomach ulcer** after **fourteen months** of illness,
   attended by a doctor, three years after the war ended, in his own house in Doorn Street. Whoever the
   testimony's murdered forebear is, **it is not Willem Hermanus.**
3. **His occupation was MASON** — a stonemason. In 1879 he was a *Boer* (farmer). See §2.
4. The informant was his eldest son **John Barry Booyzen** (b. 1884), then 21, **present at the death**.

### And the name change is visible on the page
The registering clerk writes the deceased as **BOOYSEN**. Four lines below, his son signs as
**BOOYZEN** — with an unmistakable **z**.

> **Both spellings appear on one sheet of paper in September 1905**: the father recorded under the old
> form, the son signing the new one. Three years after the Anglo-Boer War ended. That is the closest
> thing to a photograph of the name changing that this archive is ever likely to find.

## 2. Marriage register: BOOYSEN – BARRY, 22 December 1879
`sources/booyzen-photos/willem-hermanus/barry-booysen-marriage-1879.jpg` (3432 × 4042)

**"DUPLICAAT ORIGINEEL REGISTER"**, in Dutch. Huwelijk bevestigd te **Barkly**, Parochie **Barkly**,
District **Wodehouse**. Entry No. 15.

| | Groom | Bride |
|---|---|---|
| Namen en Toenamen | **Willem Hermanus Booysen** | **Catharine Mary Barry** |
| Ouderdom (age) | **22** | **21** |
| Staat | Jongman | Jongedochter |
| **Rang of Beroep** | **Boer** (farmer) | — |
| Woonplaats | **Lady Grey** | **Barkly** |
| Banns/licence | *Na Huwelijksgeboden* (after banns) | |

Getrouwd in de **Ned. Ger. Kerk te Barkly** door **David Ross**, *consulent*.
Signed by the parties: **W H Booij?en** and **C M S Barry**.
**In de tegenwoordigheid van (witnesses): J. Sutherland and P. Schoeman.**
Checked against the original register by **D. Ross**.

### Three findings
1. **He was a farmer in 1879 and a mason in 1905.** A man who works land and then works stone has, in the
   interval, very often **lost the land**. The war sits in that interval. <span>This is suggestive, not
   probative</span> — but it is exactly the trajectory the burned-farm testimony describes.
2. **A SCHOEMAN witnessed the wedding.** *P. Schoeman*, 1879, Barkly East. Set beside
   *Anna Johanna Maria Schoemann* marrying Hendrik Nicholaas Barry in 1877, and the 1901 Pretorius–Barry
   wedding held **at the home of D. Schoeman, "Caerlaverock", Barkly East** — and a Commandant D. Schoeman
   leading Cape Rebels at Labuschagne's Nek in 1900. **The Schoemans are braided through this family
   across three generations.**
3. **The groom's own signature is ambiguous and important.** The clerk wrote **Booysen**. The groom signed
   what reads as **"Booij?en"** — the older Dutch **ij** digraph, with a medial letter whose descending
   loop is more consistent with **z** than **s**.
   **If that reading is right, he was already signing with a z in 1879 — twenty years before the war —
   and the testimony's explanation of the motive is retrospective.**
   **The reading is NOT certain** at the resolution available, and cursive Dutch *s* and *z* are
   genuinely confusable. See `sources/booyzen-photos/willem-hermanus/mar-surname-zoom.png`.
   **This single letter is now the most consequential unresolved question in the archive.**

### An age problem
Age **22** in December 1879 implies birth about **1857**. The 1905 death registration gives age **55**,
implying about **1850**. The tree gives **4 May 1851** (from a DRC baptism entry). The 1879 and 1905
records disagree with each other by six years. The baptism record should outrank both.

## 3. Death notice of JOHN BARRY, dated 29 March 1915
`sources/booyzen-photos/willem-hermanus/john-barry-death-certificate.jpg` (3497 × 2798)

**Typewritten**, therefore unambiguous. Death Notice under the Administration of Estates Act 24 of 1913,
prepared by solicitors at **Barkly East**, stamped **18 April 1915**, No. 925.

**This is the very document the tree's research note describes.** It can now be quoted directly.

| Field | As typed |
|---|---|
| 1. Name in full | **John Barry** |
| 2. **Birthplace and Nationality** | **"At sea (exact whereabouts unknown), British."** |
| 3. Father | **John Barry (address unknown)** |
| 3. Mother | **Barry, born Neagle (Christian names and address unknown)** |
| 4. Age | **83 years (about)** |
| 5. **Occupation** | **Carpenter and builder** |
| 6. Residence | **Manorowen, Barkly East District** |
| 7. Condition | Married |
| 7(a) Spouse *(struck through)* | **Margaret Wilhelmina Elizabeth Barry, born Kolbe (died 2nd August 1912). In community.** |
| 8. Place of last marriage | **"Burghersdorp (probably)"** |
| 9. **Day of decease** | **3rd August 1903** |
| 9. Where died | **on farm Manorowen**, district **Barkly East** |
| 11–14. Estate | movable **Yes** · immovable **No** · exceeds £300 **Yes** · will **No** |
| Signed | **James H. Barry**, *"Son (second)"*, Barkly East, 29 March 1915 |

**Children listed (all majors, no dates given):**
John Augustus Barry · James Henry Barry · George Augustus Barry · Henry Nicholas Barry ·
Frederick Fortunatus Barry · Richard Peter Barry · **Margaret Mary Bedford, born Barry, married to
Tom Bedford** · **Kathleen Mary Boyson, born Barry, married in community of property to William Boyson**

### Four findings
1. **"At sea" is the official record, not a family guess.** The Irish/High-Seas ambiguity is real and
   contemporaneous, and the mother's maiden name **Neagle** is on a legal document.
2. **The death date is 3 August 1903, not 25 August 1903** as the tree has it. A 22-day discrepancy
   between the 1915 notice and whatever the tree drew on.
3. **"Manorowen" is one word** — the tree renders it "Manor Owen". **Manorowen is a village in
   Pembrokeshire, Wales.** A Welsh farm-name in Barkly East is a new and completely unexplored thread.
   Note too that he **owned no immovable property**: he lived on Manorowen but did not own it.
4. **A duplicate in the tree is resolved.** The tree carries both *Catherine Maria Sophia "Kathleen"
   Barry (1859–1940), married Booyzen* **and** a separate sister *Kathleen Mary Barry (Booysen)* with no
   dates. This notice lists **only one** Barry daughter married to a Boyson/Booysen. The arithmetic closes
   exactly: the tree's **11** children = the notice's **8** surviving + **2** who predeceased
   (Catherina Maria Sophia d. 1859; Willem Frederick Thomas d. 1890) + **1 duplicate**.
   ⇒ **"Kathleen Mary Barry (Booysen)" and Catherine Maria Sophia "Kathleen" Barry are the same woman.**
   The archive's earlier note about "two Barry sisters marrying Booysens" is **withdrawn**.

## 4. Death notice of GEORGE DOWNING MOUNTJOY BOOYZEN, signed 21 February 1948
`sources/booyzen-photos/george-downing-mountjoy/george-death-notice-1947.jpg` (1536 × 2048)

Death Notice under the Administration of Estates Act 1913. Master's Office stamp **1948**.

| Field | As written |
|---|---|
| Name | **George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen** |
| Birthplace and Nationality | **Cape Province, S. African** |
| Father | **Willem Hermanus Booyzen** — *deceased* |
| Mother | **Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen (Barry)** — *deceased* |
| Age | **46 years 6 months** |
| **Occupation** | **S.A.R. & H.** [South African Railways and Harbours] — role partly legible, reads as "Reception Officer" |
| Residence | **85 Rheeden Street, Forest Hill, Johannesburg** |
| Condition | Married |
| Surviving spouse | **Gertruida Alexandra Booyzen, born Kinnear — in community of property** |
| Pre-deceased spouse | **Anna Johanna Maria Booyzen (Pretorius), died 26 February 1944** |
| Place of last marriage | Johannesburg |
| **Day of decease** | **11th December 1947** |
| Where died | House **85 Rheeden St, Forest Hill**, Johannesburg |
| Children of last marriage | **"no children of last marriage"** |
| Estate | movable **Yes** · immovable **Yes** · exceeds £300 **No** · will **Yes** |
| Signed | **G. Booyzen**, Johannesburg, **21st February 1948** |

**Children of the prior marriage, as listed:**
Willem Hermanus Booyzen *(major)* · **Margareth Wilhelmina Elizabeth Kelly (born Booyzen)** ·
Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen **25/3/29** · Anna Johanna Maria Booyzen **12/10/30** ·
Georgina Jeanette Booyzen **22/9/32** · Petrus Jacobus Booyzen **10/1/34**

### Three findings
1. **HE DIED ON 11 DECEMBER 1947, NOT 21 FEBRUARY 1947.** The tree's date is wrong. **21 February is the
   date the notice was signed — in 1948.** Someone transcribing conflated the signing date with the death
   date and lost a year.
   **This dissolves the archive's own "ten-month gap to burial" anomaly**: he died 11 December and was
   buried at West Park on **14 December 1947 — three days later**, exactly as one would expect.
2. **His occupation was the South African Railways and Harbours**, not the mines. The testimony's "the
   boys all got jobs on the mines" refers to an earlier generation; by 1947 this branch was on the
   railways.
3. **Margaretha Wilhelmina Elizabeth "Maggie" Booyzen (b. 1926) had married a KELLY** by February 1948.
   The tree records no married name for her.
4. The four minors' birth dates on the notice **match the tree exactly** — an independent confirmation of
   Catherine Mary Sophia's birth on **25 March 1929**.

## 5. Photographs

### `george-and-isaak-pretorius-transkei.jpg` (2303 × 3000) — captioned
Two men shaking hands in open veld. **Left:** a younger man, early-to-mid thirties, in a two-piece suit
with the wide trousers of the 1930s — **George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen**. **Right:** an elderly man with
a white moustache in a three-piece suit, a walking stick planted in the grass behind him —
**Isaak Pretorius**, a relative of George's wife. Captioned in the tree as **"In Transkei"**.
**This appears to be the only photograph of George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen in the tree** — his profile
still carries MyHeritage's "Hint: Personal photo", meaning none has been set.

### Three uncaptioned photographs — identifications are INFERRED, not documented
- **`untitled-43.jpg`** — a young woman in a patterned 1930s dress, an **elderly woman seated in full
  black**, and a young man in a light jacket, outside a plastered house with a corrugated roof.
  <span>Hypothesis</span> the seated woman is **Catherine Maria Sophia "Kathleen" Barry (1859–1940)**,
  George's mother, in widow's black. Dress and print date the image to the 1930s, when she was in her
  seventies and living in Johannesburg. **Unverified.**
- **`untitled-45.jpg`** — a young man in a **dark uniform tunic and peaked cap**, in a yard with a bare
  tree. <span>Hypothesis</span> **George Downing Mountjoy Booyzen in South African Railways and Harbours
  uniform** — which his 1947 death notice gives as his employer. **Unverified.**
- **`untitled-46.jpg`** — a man in a light suit and fedora leaning on a late-1920s sedan, registration
  **TAS 486**, with a small boy in a cap and shorts, beside a corrugated-iron and brick building.
  The registration is a hard, researchable detail. **Unidentified.**
