# Research queue

Ordered by what each would unlock. Mirrors `site/src/pages/open-questions.astro`.

## § 0 — BLOCKING · Who were Gerthardus Lodewikus Booysen's parents?
The male line stops dead seven generations up: no dates, no father, no mother.
**Look in:** Cape DRC baptism registers, Graaff-Reinet and Paarl, 1740–1790 · the published
*Suid-Afrikaanse Geslagsregisters* (SAG) Booysen genealogy · the MOOC estate papers of
Magdelena Elisabeth De Beer, d. 1843.

## § 1 — HIGH · Is there a Booysen on a Cape Rebel roll?
**Look in:** the three-volume Cape Rebel registers (c. 15,433 names) · Special Tribunal and military
court records from Oct 1900 · Barkly East and Dordrecht commando rolls.

## § 2 — HIGH · Who was "Commandant D. Schoeman"?
The family married in his house at "Caerlaverock", Barkly East, 8 Jul 1901. A Commandant D. Schoeman
led ~400 rebels at Labuschagne's Nek in Mar 1900. The bride's mother was a Schoemann.
**Look in:** rebel commandant lists · Barkly East treason files · the marriage register entry itself
(it should name witnesses).

## § 3 — HIGH · Were the women and children in a camp?
The most answerable claim in the testimony, and nobody has looked.
**Look in:** ABW concentration camp registers, indexed by name — Booysen, Booyzen and Barry;
Kathleen Barry b. 1859 with children born 1884–1899.

## § 4 — HIGH · When did Willem Hermanus Booyzen actually die?
Tree gives 13 Sep 1905 at Indwe and doubts itself; a son is recorded born 1907.
**Look in:** NAAIRS / KAB MOOC death notices for Booysen and Booyzen, 1899–1910 · Indwe and Barkly East
DRC burial registers.
**This single document would change more than any further reasoning.**

## § 5 — HIGH · Was John Augustus Barry Irish, English, or born at sea?
**Look in:** the two 1903 death certificates and the 1915 document named in the tree note · Cape Mounted
Rifles records at Johnson's Leap · Cork Nagle/Neagle baptisms c.1819 · 1819–20 passenger lists.

## § 6 — HIGH · Break the John Barry (1796–1853) conflation
**Look in:** 1841 and 1851 England censuses, Holly Bower, Bilsdale · St Hilda's Bilsdale burial register ·
the Cape death record for de Bruins Poort.

## § 7 — MEDIUM · Who was Petrus Jacobus Booysen's second wife?
**Look in:** Cape marriage registers, Cradock and Middelburg, 1863–1875 · the 1884 estate file, which
should name a surviving widow.

# UPDATE — 9 September 2026, after the document extraction

**§ 4 is ANSWERED.** The 1905 death registration was in the tree's own photo store. Willem Hermanus
Booysen died 13 Sep 1905, Doorn Street, Indwe, **ulcer of the stomach**, fourteen months' illness,
Dr W. C. Macdonald; informant **J B Booyzen**, present at death; occupation **Mason**; twelve children.
Registered 14 Sep 1905, entry 299, sub-district Indwe, district Wodehouse.

**§ 5 is PARTLY ANSWERED.** John Barry's 1915 death notice is read: *"At sea (exact whereabouts unknown),
British"*, mother *"born Neagle"*, occupation carpenter and builder, farm **Manorowen**, no immovable
property, died **3 Aug 1903** (not 25 Aug), place of last marriage *"Burghersdorp (probably)"*.

**NEW § 4 (replacing the old one) — the letter in the 1879 signature.**
The clerk wrote *Booysen*; the groom appears to have signed *Booijzen*. Needs the ORIGINAL register at
the Cape Archives (this is a duplicate) and an eye trained on 19th-century Cape Dutch hands.

**NEW findings to chase**
- **Manorowen** — Welsh village name (Pembrokeshire) used for a Barkly East farm. Unexplained.
- **Margaretha Wilhelmina Elizabeth "Maggie" Booyzen married a KELLY** before Feb 1948.
- **George's employer: S.A.R. & H.** — South African Railways and Harbours. Try SAR&H staff registers.
- **Vehicle registration TAS 486** on an uncaptioned c.1929 sedan.
- **P. Schoeman** witnessed the 1879 Booysen–Barry marriage — third Schoeman tie.

**§ 8 upgraded to HIGH.** The photo store is not photographs, it is a document archive. George Augustus
Kolbe alone has **23 images and 4 biographies**, still unread. Extraction method: each MyHeritage photo
page embeds `originalPhotoParams.src` — a full-resolution signed URL — in its HTML.
