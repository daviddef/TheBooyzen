# The Barry marriage — where the English names come from

## Catherine Maria Sophia "Kathleen" Barry (1859–1940)
- b. **4 Aug 1859, Barkly East**; christened **Sep 1859, Dordrecht** (Cape)
  - src: FamilySearch ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSV8-85FP i=513 cat=968412
- **Occupation recorded: "Needle Woman"** — a rare and valuable social detail; this is not a gentry marriage
- m. 22 Dec 1879 — Willem Hermanus "William Henry" Booyzen
- d. **5 Sep 1940, Mayfair, Johannesburg**
- MyHeritage: 8 photos, **11 sources**, 1 Smart Match, Geni World Family Tree saved record

### The name she was given
Her sister **Catherina Maria Sophia Barry, b. 1855, d. 1859** — died the year Catherine was born.
Catherine was given the dead child's name. A necronym: standard practice, and still a small grief
preserved in a name that then travelled three more generations, to **Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen**
(1929–2013), David Defranceski's grandmother.

### Mayfair closes a circle
Kathleen Barry died at **Mayfair, Johannesburg** in 1940. Nine years later, in **1949**, her
granddaughter and namesake Catherine Mary Sophia Booyzen married Nuno Fernando Lerena at
**St Joseph's Catholic Church, Mayfair**. The family that came off the train from the Cape stayed put.

## Her parents — the Barry–Kolbe marriage
- **John Augustus Barry I** (1819–1903)
- **Margaretha Wilhelmina Elizabeth Mary Kolbe** (1826–1912)
  ⇒ **This is the source of "David Kolbe Booyzen" (1896–1980)** — the Kolbe surname carried forward
    as a forename, the standard Cape way of keeping a maternal line visible.
  ⇒ Margaretha had an earlier marriage to an **Enslin**: half-brothers John Adrian Enslin (1851–1897),
    Augustas **Kolbe** Enslin (1853–1937), Harry Pieter Enslin (1861–1919). Note "Kolbe" used as a
    forename there too.

## Two Barry sisters married Booysens
**Kathleen Mary Barry** is recorded with the married name **(Booysen)** — a second Barry–Booysen
marriage in the same sibling group. And in the next generation **Henry James Booyzen** married
**Dorothea Regina Barry** (17 Jul 1912, **Elliot, Cape Province**) — a third Barry–Booyzen tie.
These two families married each other repeatedly. That is a small-district pattern.

## A stray line resolved
The odd fragment "Spouse: Maria Jacoba Kolbe-Booysen (born Smit)" seen on Catherine (1929–2013)'s
profile is explained: **David Kolbe Booyzen married Maria Jacoba Smit, 23 Aug 1921, Johannesburg.**
She is recorded elsewhere under the compound "Kolbe-Booysen."
