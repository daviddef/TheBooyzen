**Subject:** Request for copies — six estate files, Master of the Supreme Court, Transvaal (MHG), 1923–1957

---

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing from Australia to ask for copies of six estate files held at the National Archives
Repository. I have the full references for all six, which I hope makes this straightforward. I
should be grateful for guidance on your procedure, your fees, and any identification you require.

All six belong to one family — the Booyzen family, who moved from the north-eastern Cape to
Johannesburg in the first decades of the twentieth century — and I am assembling a documented
family history rather than making a legal claim.

## 1. The files, in order of priority

**TAB, MHG 1095/48 (1947)**
> BOOYZEN, GEORGE DOWNING MOUNTJOY
Died 11 December 1947 at Forest Hill, Johannesburg. A clerk with the South African Railways.
His wife's file, MHG 2945/44, I have already read.

**TAB, MHG 3734/46 (1946)**
> DASCHNER, CATHERINE MARY SOPHIA, born BOOYZEN
Born 22 April 1891, baptised at Barkly Oost. Married Joseph Heinrich Daschner at Indwe; his
estate is MHG 1928/42, which I have read. Hers should name her parents, which is what I am after.

**TAB, MHG 769/52 (1952)**
> BOOYZEN, JOHN BARRY
Born about 1884. He signed his father's death registration at Indwe in 1905.

**TAB, MHG 5155/51 (1951)**
> BOOYZEN, GERT JACOBUS
Born 19 January 1870; married Isabella Frederika Coetsee at Middelburg in 1896.

**TAB, MHG 10/57 (1956)**
> BOOYZEN, HENRY JAMES

**TAB, MHG 52653 (1923)**
> BOOYZEN, JACOBUS NICHOLS
The eldest of the eight children named on an 1863 Cape death notice. This is the one file of the
six that I have been unable to locate on film at all, so if the reference needs correcting I
should be glad to know it.

## 2. What I am asking for

For each file, in order of usefulness to me:

1. **The death notice (sterfkennis)** — this is the page I most want, for the parents,
   the birthplace, the occupation and the list of children.
2. **The liquidation and distribution account**, if the file has one.
3. **The will**, if the file has one.

If copying whole files is impractical, the death notice alone from each would be of enormous
value, and I would rather have six death notices than one complete file.

## 3. Practical

I am happy to pay reproduction and postage fees, and to receive scans by email rather than paper
by post if that is simpler. If you would prefer that I engage a local researcher to attend the
Reading Room on my behalf, I would welcome a suggestion of how to go about that.

I am conscious that requests from overseas take staff time that could go to readers in front of
you, and I am grateful for whatever you are able to do.
