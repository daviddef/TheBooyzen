**Subject:** Three references — VAB A1 19 No. 244, VAB MHG M777 and VAB MHG K71 (KOLBE)

Dear Mr Challa,

I am researching the family of **John Gerson Eliezar Kolbe**, a commandant in the Orange
Free State who died at the farm **Leeuwberg on 3 April 1868**, and I should be grateful for
copies of three items held at VAB. I have put them in the order they matter to me, and I am
glad to take them one at a time if that is easier.

**1. VAB, A1 19, Renier-versameling, No. 244 — *Herinneringe van genl. W J Kolbe*.**
I should like **a copy of page 78**, and, if the item is short enough to make it reasonable,
**of the whole of it** — together with **its extent** (how many pages) and, if the finding
aid records one, **a date and the name of the person who wrote it**.

I know of this only from a footnote: **Fransjohan Pretorius, "Straf op Kommando gedurende
die Anglo-Boereoorlog, 1899–1902", *Military History Journal* Vol. 8 No. 3**, which cites it
at page 78. *(The journal prints the reference as "Al 19"; I have read it as **A1 19**, and
if that is wrong I should be glad to be told.)* **What I am trying to establish is whether
"genl. W J Kolbe" is Willem Johannes Kolbe (1847–1941)**, who was born in the district of
Philippolis and led the Bloemfontein commando. I am aware that a *Renier-versameling* also
appears at the National Archives in Pretoria, listing correspondence with a **"P Kolbe"**, so
the collector plainly dealt with more than one member of this family — which is exactly why
I would rather see the document than assume.

**2. VAB MHG M777 — McCABE, ANNA JACOBA, née VAN DER MERWE, wife of JOHN GHERSON [KOLBE],
1896.** The full file if possible; **if the file is large, the death notice alone would be a
great deal.** She was the commandant's widow and remarried, so her estate is filed under her
second husband's surname. **His obituary says he left eight sons and four daughters, and I
can name only two of them.** A death notice listing the surviving children would answer a
question this archive has not been able to answer any other way.

**3. VAB MHG K71 — KOLBE, JOHN GERSON ELEAZAR, spouse ANNA JACOBA (born VAN DER MERWE),
1868.** His own estate. Again, **the death notice is the page I most want** if the whole file
is impractical.

**One question rather than a request.** I have **several further references at VAB** —
another estate file, an insolvent estate, a 1931 court application naming six Kolbes
together, and the Volksraad minutes for May 1867. **Would you prefer those in this same
order, or as a separate enquiry later?** I do not want to hand you a long list when a short
one would be answered sooner, and I am content to wait.

Please let me know the **reproduction fees and how you would like to be paid**. I am writing
from Australia, so I will arrange whatever suits your finance office rather than the other
way round. If any of these references is wrong or does not resolve, **telling me so would be
a complete and welcome answer** — it would save you searching and save me waiting.

With thanks for your time,

David
