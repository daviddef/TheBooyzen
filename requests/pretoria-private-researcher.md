# Sent — the private researcher at Pretoria, and the reply to NARSSA

**Status: BOTH SENT, 15 September 2026.** This is the first paid-research approach
this archive has made. Everything before it has been a request to an institution.

## How it came about

The letter of 12 September to `enquiries2@dsac.gov.za` asked the National Archives
for copies of six Transvaal estate files. It was forwarded internally to the Reading
Room and answered on 15 September by **Ms Mavis Xaba, Archivist Assistant**:

> "Please note that all the estate files you are requesting are **bound files**.
> Unfortunately, we are therefore unable to scan these files and provide complete
> digital copies by email."

She put a private researcher on copy — `digitalphoto@icon.co.za` — described as
specialising in estate files and recommended by a number of researchers who have
used her. **The obstacle is the binding, not the willingness**, and that is a
physical fact about the record rather than a policy.

## 1 — Reply to Ms Xaba (sent in thread)

Thanks, and one question only: whether **TAB MHG 52653** looks like a valid
reference, since it is written without a year suffix unlike the other five and is
the one file of the six never located on microfilm. Framed as ignorable.

## 2 — To the researcher (new thread)

**Subject:** Estate file research, National Archives Pretoria — six MHG files,
1923–1957 (referred by Ms Mavis Xaba)

The six files in priority order, and for each: the **death notice first**, then the
liquidation and distribution account, then the will. Photographs accepted; they do
not need to be flat or square.

**What was asked before anything else:** whether she takes the work, and what she
charges and how — per file, per page, hourly, call-out. Her view on how much is
worth doing at all. An offer to start with one file.

**MHG 52653 was explicitly deprioritised**: "Please do not spend time hunting it.
If it is not where it should be, leaving it is the right answer."

**Further work flagged, so she can judge scale:** the seven CJC war-compensation
files for Marico, 1903–04; the OD education files of 1898; and two Free State
estates at Bloemfontein if VAB is a depot she works in — and a name if it is not.

**No payment details were sent and none will be.** The letter asks her to name her
own arrangement. Paying is the user's to do.
