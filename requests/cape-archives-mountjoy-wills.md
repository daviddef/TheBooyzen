## 2. Why these three

James Mountjoy was a saddler at Cradock who died there in September 1827. His death notice —
**MOOC 6/9/21, reference 4393**, which I have already read — was completed thirteen years after his
death, and against *"Names of the Parents of the Deceased"* it says only **"Not Known."** His
birthplace is given as **"Europe"**, with no country.

His marriage register entry, Graaff-Reinet, 9 March 1817, records no place of origin for him either,
on a page where other couples are entered *"beide van Uitenhage"*.

So a will of **1818**, made a year after that marriage, and a **Colonial Office memorial** written by his
widow in her own cause, are the two document types most likely to state where he came from. That is the
whole of my interest in them. He is my wife's — my — direct ancestor, and this is the oldest point in
the family that any record reaches.
