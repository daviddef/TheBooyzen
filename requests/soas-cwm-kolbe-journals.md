**Subject:** Copies from CWM/LMS — four journals of George Augustus Kolbe, Philippolis, 1828–1834

Dear Special Collections,

I am researching **George Augustus Kolbe (1802–1844)**, an 1820 Settler who served the London
Missionary Society in southern Africa from 1829 to 1837, latterly at Philippolis. He is my
wife's family's ancestor, and I am assembling a documented family history rather than working
towards a publication.

Your catalogue is item-level for this material, so I can give you references rather than ask
you to search. **I should be grateful for a quotation for digital copies of the following, and
for guidance on your procedure, fees and any permissions.**

## The journals — these are the priority

| Reference | Description |
|---|---|
| **CWM/LMS/05/05/100** | Extracts from the Journal of **John Melvill and George Augustus Kolbe**, during a tour to the north-eastwards of Philippolis, **13–22 Nov 1828** |
| **CWM/LMS/05/05/102** | Extracts from the Journal of George Augustus Kolbe, Philippolis, **1828–1831** |
| **CWM/LMS/05/05/106** | Journal of George Augustus Kolbe, Philippolis, **1831–1834** |
| **CWM/LMS/05/05/108** | Journal of George Augustus Kolbe, Philippolis, **6 Oct 1832 – Sep 1833** |

The first of these was printed in an edited form in the Society's *Transactions* for 1829, and
is cited in the modern literature as an extract addressed to the Rev Richard Miles, dated at
Philippolis 25 November 1828. **I have never seen either the printed version or the
manuscript.** If it is simplest to begin with one item so that we both see how it goes, please
start with CWM/LMS/05/05/100.

## Incoming correspondence — lower priority, but one year matters

| Reference | Description |
|---|---|
| **CWM/LMS/05/02/02/044** | South Africa, 1835 — your catalogue lists **George Augustus Kolbe, Philippolis** among the correspondents |
| **CWM/LMS/05/02/02/046** | South Africa, 1836 (Box 15) |
| — | the **1837** file in the same box |

I ask for 1836 and 1837 for a particular reason, and I would rather state it plainly. A draft
Dictionary of South African Biography entry on Kolbe, by the late **Karel Schoeman**, records
that in 1836 he was **accused of adultery** with the wife of a member of his congregation; that
**H. Hendricks**, secretary of the Griqua Council, led the agitation; that **an inquiry by the
neighbouring French missionaries acquitted him**; and that he withdrew from the station in
March 1837, his connection with the Society being dissolved by the Directors on 19 June 1837 —
which last your own register confirms. **I would like to read what the Society was actually
told, rather than repeat a summary of it.** If those two files are bulky, I would be glad
simply to know whether they contain material on the matter before committing to copying.

## One further question, which needs no searching

Sibree's *Register of Missionaries* (1923) describes **Frederick William Kolbe** (entry 522) as
"son of Rev. G. A. Kolbe (No. 278)". **I believe this to be an error** — other sources make him
a Westphalian German of the Rhenish Mission, and the two birth dates are only eight months
apart, which is impossible. **If the Society holds candidate papers for Frederick William
Kolbe on his acceptance in 1853, they would state his parentage and settle it.** I do not ask
you to look; I ask only whether such papers survive as a class, so that I know whether to
pursue it.
