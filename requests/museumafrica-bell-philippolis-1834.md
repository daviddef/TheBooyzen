Museum, but the specific Philippolis 1834 sheet cannot be confirmed from here. The museum

person on the spot. This is a letter because nothing else will do.

**What it would produce.** This archive holds **no likeness of anybody in it born before

**Subject:** A Charles Bell watercolour of a church service at Philippolis, 1834 — enquiry about an original

Dear MuseumAfrica,

I am researching **George Augustus Kolbe (1802–1844)**, a London Missionary Society
missionary at Philippolis, and I am trying to trace one picture.

**What I am looking for.** A watercolour by **Charles Davidson Bell** showing a church
service at **Philippolis in 1834**, with Kolbe in the pulpit. It is reproduced in **Percival
R. Kirby / Frank Lye (ed.), *The Journal of Andrew Smith* (Balkema, Cape Town, 1975)**, and
the *Dictionary of South African Biography* credits the original to the **Africana Museum,
Johannesburg** — your predecessor institution.

**My three questions, in order of usefulness to me.**

1. **Does MuseumAfrica still hold this watercolour**, and if so, what is its accession or
   catalogue number?
2. **Is a reproduction available** — a photograph or scan — and what would it cost? I am
   happy to pay a reproduction fee and to observe any credit line you require.
3. If the sheet is **not** yours, do you know where it went? Collections of this kind have
   sometimes been transferred, and a pointer would be as valuable to me as the picture.

**Two things that may help you find it.** Bell made some 300 watercolours and drawings on
Dr Andrew Smith's expedition of 1834–36, and I understand a large number of the originals
came to the Africana Museum. The Philippolis sheet is one of a group of mission-station
subjects: the same series is separately represented in Scotland, at the **University of
Edinburgh, Centre for the Study of World Christianity, CSWC 60**, as five photograph copies
of Bell sketches of African congregations — Kolbe at Philippolis, **James Archbell**,
**William Edwards**, **Jean-Pierre Pellissier**, and a service at **Ebenezer**. If your
records group them the same way, the other four names may find the file faster than Kolbe
does.

**I should say plainly what this is for.** I keep a family history archive that publishes
its sources and its failures alongside its findings. It is not commercial and nothing is
sold from it. If a reproduction is not possible, **a catalogue reference alone would be a
real gain** — it would let me cite the picture properly instead of citing a book that cites
it.

I am writing from Australia, so I cannot call in, and I am grateful for whatever time you
can give this.

With thanks,

David
