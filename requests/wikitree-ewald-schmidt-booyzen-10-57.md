**Subject:** Henry James Booyzen (Booyzen-12) — your death notice source, and some Cape records to trade

Dear Mr Schmidt,

I manage a small evidence-first family archive for the Booyzen family of Graaff-Reinet,
Cradock, Barkly East and Johannesburg, and I have been reading your WikiTree profiles with
real gratitude. I am writing about one source, and offering several in return, because it
seems only fair to arrive with something in hand.

## What I am asking about

Your profile for **Henry James Booyzen (Booyzen-12)** lists as source [2] a
**"Form of information of death"**.

If that is the death notice from his Transvaal estate file — **TAB, MHG 10/57** — then you
have something I have not been able to reach. That file sits on a FamilySearch film that
refuses to serve images: film 007830063 and every film after it in collection 2520237
returns an error, and those are exactly the films carrying 1946 and later. I have a letter
in with the National Archives Repository in Pretoria about it, along with five others, and
I do not expect a quick answer.

What I am after from it is the ordinary thing: **his parents as the form names them, his
birthplace, his occupation, and the list of children**. Even a transcription of those four
lines would be enormous. I am not asking you to do any work you have not already done — if
you simply have a scan or a note, that is more than enough, and if you would rather just
tell me where you found it, that would help too.

## What I can offer in return

These are all from documents I have read, and I am happy to send images of any of them.

**1. Hendrik Stefanus Booysen (your Booysen-293) — his mother is settled.** Your profile
reads *"Elizabetha ? (Anna Maria ?) van der Merwe"*, and the question mark is the right
instinct. His own death notice, **TAB estate 14380**, names his mother as
**"Elizabetha Booysen, born van der Merwe"**. Petrus Jacobus married two van der Merwe
women in succession: Anna Maria died before March 1825, and Hendrik Stefanus was born about
June 1828 — his notice gives his age at death as 79 years and 11 months. The structured
parent link on the profile currently points at Anna Maria.

**2. Charlotta Elizabetha Dormehl**, his second wife, whom you have as 1841–1902: born
about October 1841 at Graaff-Reinet, died **6 February 1902 at Driefontein, Groot Marico**.
She appears in the records under two married surnames, Roos and Booysen.

**3. Henry James Booyzen's death, from the Johannesburg cemetery register.** Died
8 December 1956 at the Florence Nightingale Nursing Home of coronary pulmonary sclerosis,
aged 69; usual residence 3 15th Avenue, Mayfair. Buried 12 December, Dutch Reformed
section, **grave 7110/1**, a double plot.

**4. A correction you may want.** Your **Booyzen-2**, Hendrik Stiphanis Booyzen of
Graaff-Reinet, has a first marriage at Worcester on 28 December 1842 recorded against a
birth of 27 June 1830 — which would make him twelve. I mention it only because it is the
sort of thing that is invisible once it has been in a tree for a while. He is a different
family from mine and I have no other stake in it.

## One disagreement I would genuinely like your view on

Your **Booysen-267** — Petrus Jacobus Booysen, born before 21 September 1788, died
1 September 1876 — is certainly my man; the dates match exactly. You give his parents as
**Jan (Boijens) Booysen and Magdalena de Beer**.

I have read **his 1876 Rustenburg death notice, No. 678**, and line 3 of it names his
parents as **"Gerthardus Lödewikus Booijsen" and "Magdalena de Beer"**. Same mother, same
son, a different husband.

I am not assuming you are wrong. There is a 1793 Cape notarial will, **KAB NCD 2/1: 49**,
for *"Jan Booysen, married to the late Magdalena de Beer"*, which I had set aside as the
wrong man — and your tree puts that same Jan in exactly the position I declined to put him.
I would very much like to know whether your Jan comes from **Heese, Suid-Afrikaanse
Geslagsregisters, Deel 1, p. 331**, which your profile cites, and if so what that page
actually says. I do not have access to the book from here, and that page reference is the
most useful thing anyone has given me on this question in months.

If it turns out the Sterfkennis and Heese disagree, that is worth both of us knowing.
