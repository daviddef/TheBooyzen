# G2G post — WikiTree, estate MHG 10/57 and the Booyzen line

**Status: POST 1 IS ANSWERED** — checked 14 September 2026. **Two answers in eighteen hours,
from Pieter van Zyl (G2G-4), five upvotes each.**

1. The **"Form of information of death"** cited on Booyzen-12 is the *Aangifte van 'n
   Sterfgeval* — the state registration form — and **does not carry parents, spouse or
   children**. The *sterfkennis* is the other document, the one attached to the probate.
   **So nobody has already read the page this archive wants**, and the Pretoria letter is
   still the only route to MHG 10/57. That closes the question the post was asked to close.
2. He **uploaded the baptism** to the profile: FamilySearch ark `1:1:6KPT-T3Z4`, born
   12 Aug 1887, baptised 27 Nov 1887, parents Willem Hermanus Booijsen and Catharina Maria
   Sophia Barry. Verified here against the index.
3. Unverified, and recorded as unverified: *"a carpenter when married and a retired miner of
   Langlaagte Estates when he died of Phthisis."* This archive has **coronary pulmonary
   sclerosis** off the burial register.

## POST 1 — the one asked for

**Category:** Genealogy Help
**Tags:** `south_africa` `south_african_roots` `booysen` `death_notice`

**Title:** Has anyone seen the sterfkennis in Transvaal estate MHG 10/57 (Booyzen, 1956)?

I am after the death notice from **TAB, MHG 10/57** — the Transvaal estate of
**Henry James Booyzen**, who died at Johannesburg on 8 December 1956. The estate number is
a 1957 one, which is why the file is sometimes cited as 1957.

The reason I cannot simply read it: it is in FamilySearch collection 2520237, and that
collection stops serving images partway through. Film **007830063** and every film after it
returns an error, and those are exactly the films carrying 1946 and later. Twenty-eight
tested, twenty-eight refused. I have a written request in with the National Archives
Repository in Pretoria for this file and five others, and I am not expecting a fast answer.

What prompted this post is that the profile [[Booyzen-12]] lists among its sources a
**"Form of information of death"**, which reads like this very document. If that is right,
somebody has already had it in front of them.

**What I am hoping for, in order of usefulness:**

1. His **parents** as the form names them.
2. His **birthplace** and his **occupation**.
3. The **list of children**.

A transcription of those lines would be plenty — I am not asking anyone to hunt down a
scan. And if the "Form of information of death" turns out to be a different document
altogether, I would still like to know which, because that is useful to me too.

**What I can already put against it**, from the Johannesburg cemetery register: he died at
the Florence Nightingale Nursing Home of coronary pulmonary sclerosis, aged 69; usual
residence 3 15th Avenue, Mayfair; buried 12 December in the Dutch Reformed section, grave
7110/1, a double plot. He was a son of Willem Hermanus Booyzen and Catherine Maria Sophia
Barry, and in 1912 he married Dorothea Regina Barry, his mother's brother's daughter.

Happy to share images of anything I hold on this family in return.

## POST 2 — REWRITTEN 13 Sep after the Boijens-9 / De_Beer-638 finding

The original draft asked "is the father Jan or Gerthardus Lodewikus?". That question
answered itself an hour later: Jan remarried on 15 December 1793, his de Beer wife was dead
by then, ours died in 1843, and the two Magdalenas are merged on one WikiTree profile which
flags its own doubt. So the post below asks the question that is actually still open — WHICH
de Beer daughter is ours — and asks for two printed pages rather than one.

**Category:** Genealogy Help
**Tags:** `south_africa` `south_african_roots` `booysen` `de_beer`

**Title:** Two Magdalena de Beers (b6c2 and b6c6) — which one married the Booysen, and what do Heese p. 331 and de Villiers p. 78 say?

I am trying to separate two women, and I think a WikiTree profile has them merged. I would
be grateful to anyone with either of the printed genealogies to hand.

**The profile.** De_Beer-638 carries the code **"b6c2 (and b6c6 — see Research Note)"**, two
baptisms nine years apart — **16 October 1745** at Tulbagh and **16 October 1754** at
Stellenbosch — and a parents' children list that holds **both a Magdalena de Beer and a
Magdalena Elizabeth de Beer**, daughters of Zaccharias de Beer and Anna Pretorius.

**Why it matters to me.** The merge appears to have put the wrong husband over my line.

- **Boijens-9**, Jan Booysen, baptised Drakenstein 29 January 1741, married Magdalena de
  Beer on 21 January 1770 — and **married again on 15 December 1793**. So his de Beer wife
  was dead by the end of 1793. That also identifies the Cape notarial will **KAB NCD 2/1: 49**,
  *"Jan Booysen, married to the late Magdalena de Beer"*, as his.
- **My** Magdelena Elisabeth de Beer **died in 1843**, fifty years later, and was the wife of
  **Gerthardus Lodewikus Booysen**. I have that from a primary document: line 3 of the 1876
  Rustenburg sterfkennis, No. 678, of their son Petrus Jacobus Booysen (1788–1876), which
  names both parents.
- **Booysen-267** nonetheless gives that same Petrus Jacobus the father **Jan**, which I
  think is the merge showing through.

**What I am asking for:**

1. **Heese, *Suid-Afrikaanse Geslagsregisters*, Deel 1, p. 331** — cited on Booysen-267.
   What does it give as Petrus Jacobus Booysen's father?
2. **de Villiers and Pama, *Genealogies of Old Cape Families*, Vol 1, Booijsen, p. 78** —
   cited on Boijens-9. Does it distinguish b6c2 from b6c6, and does a Gerthardus Lodewikus
   Booysen appear at all?

I have neither book where I am. If the two printed sources disagree with the sterfkennis,
that is worth knowing on its own, and I am happy to send an image of line 3 to anyone who
wants to read it themselves.
