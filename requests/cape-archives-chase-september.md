Dear Sir or Madam,

I wrote on 9 and 10 September with two small copy requests and I am following up gently, in
case either message went astray rather than into a queue. I do not want to chase work that
is simply in hand.

**The two requests were:**

1. **MOOC 7/1/104, refs 42 and 43**, and **CO 3972, ref 17** — the Mountjoy and Slier wills
   of 1818 and 1827, and an 1834 memorial to the Colonial Office from "the widow of J
   Mountjoy".
2. **MOOC 6/9/17017, ref 1847/50** — the death notice of **Robert Paul Lerena**, died 19
   March 1950.

**If it would help, I am glad to make this smaller.** If the whole of MOOC 7/1/104 refs 42
and 43 is a large order, **the single most useful page to me is the first page of each will**
— the one naming the testator, the date, and the executors. I can ask for the remainder later
if it proves worth it. The same goes for CO 3972 ref 17: **the memorial's first page and
signature** would answer my question on its own.

**And if any of these references is wrong, please do say so.** Three of the four came from
NAAIRS and one from a secondary citation, and a reference that does not resolve is worth
knowing about quickly — it would save you looking and save me waiting. **That answer would
close the enquiry as completely as the documents would.**

Please also let me know the current fee and how you would prefer payment; I am in Australia,
so I will arrange whatever suits your finance office rather than the other way round.

With thanks for your time, and with no wish to hurry a reading room that is doing other
people's work as well as mine,

David
