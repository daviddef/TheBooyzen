**Subject:** Request for copies — six deceased estate files, Master of the Supreme Court, Natal (MSCE), 1956–1974

---

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing from Australia to ask for copies of six deceased estate files held at the
Pietermaritzburg Archives Repository. I have the full MSCE references for all six, taken
from NAAIRS, which I hope makes this straightforward. I should be grateful for guidance on
your procedure, your fees, and any identification you require.

I am assembling a documented family history rather than making any legal claim. The files
fall into three groups, and I have put them in the order that would help me most.

## 1. Two Kolbe men of Wakkerstroom — the files I most want

Both were born in the Wakkerstroom district of the eastern Transvaal and died in Natal. I
am trying to establish whether the Wakkerstroom Kolbes descend from **George Augustus
Kolbe**, an 1820 Settler who died at Colesberg in 1844 — a question two forenames and one
surname strongly suggest but no document yet states.

**NAB, MSCE 2032/1969**
> KOLBE, FREDERIK FORTUNATUS HOFMEYR. BORN IN NAAUWPOORT, WAKKERSTROOM.
Spouse born Badenhorst. *Frederik Fortunatus* is the Settler's son's name, carried into a
fourth generation.

**NAB, MSCE 965/1956**
> KOLBE, HENDRIK JOHANNES HOFMEYR. BORN IN WAKKERSTROOM.

## 2. Two Kolbe-born women of Colesberg

Since drafting this I have established who both women are, from Dutch Reformed baptism
registers: they are daughters of **Frederik Fortunatus Kolbe** (born Bethulie 1839, the
Settler's tenth child) and **Elizabeth Johanna du Toit**. What these files should give me
is the end of each woman's life, and the children.

**NAB, MSCE 131/1959**
> BEUKES, PETRONELLA JACOBA ADRIANA. BORN KOLBE. BORN IN COLESBERG.
Baptised at Colesberg, 18 November 1877; born 2 October 1877.

**NAB, MSCE 414/1960**
> OOSTHUIZEN, PIERRE FRANCOIS. BORN COLESBERG. Spouse ADRIANA HENRIETTA JOHANNA, BORN KOLBE.
She was baptised at Bethulie, 23 October 1881; born 12 August 1881. **Her husband's file,
not hers** — so if only one page can be copied, the one naming the spouse and children is
what I need.

## 3. Two people born at Barkly East

Barkly East, in the north-eastern Cape, is the town this family farmed in from about 1859.
Both of these are people who left it for Natal, and neither is yet in my records.

**NAB, MSCE 2183/1958**
> BARRY, HENRY JAMES KOLBE. BORN IN BARKLY EAST, CAPE PROVINCE. UNMARRIED.
This man carries a Barry forename and a Kolbe surname together, which is the exact
conjunction I am trying to document. As he died unmarried, his file should name parents or
siblings rather than a widow.

**NAB, MSCE 600/1974**
> MOUNTJOY, CHRISTIAN. BORN IN BARKLY EAST. Surviving spouse a Mountjoy.

## 4. What I am asking for

For each file, in order of usefulness to me:

1. **The death notice (sterfkennis)** — the page I most want, for the parents, the
   birthplace, the occupation and the list of children.
2. **The liquidation and distribution account**, if the file has one.
3. **The will**, if the file has one.

If copying whole files is impractical, **the death notice alone from each would be of
enormous value**, and I would rather have six death notices than one complete file.

## 5. Practical

I am happy to pay reproduction and postage fees, and to receive scans by email rather than
paper by post if that is simpler. If you would prefer that I engage a local researcher to
attend the Reading Room on my behalf, I would welcome a suggestion of how to go about that.

I am conscious that requests from overseas take staff time that could go to readers in
front of you, and I am grateful for whatever you are able to do.
